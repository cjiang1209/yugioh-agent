# Extra Monster Zone Rules (Rulebook v10)

## Layout

- There are 2 Extra Monster Zones (EMZ) on the field, one on each side at the TOP of the board (above the 5 Main Monster Zones).
- They are SHARED zones — both sit in the middle of the combined field, accessible to both players.
- Each player can normally use only 1 of the 2 EMZs.
- A monster placed in an EMZ does NOT count toward the 5-monster limit of the Main Monster Zone.

## What goes there

- Monsters Summoned from the Extra Deck (Link, Synchro, Fusion, Xyz) normally must go to the EMZ.
- If a Link Monster is on the field and points to a Main Monster Zone, Extra Deck monsters can also be placed in those pointed-to Main Monster Zones instead.

## Key rules

- Each player can normally use 1 EMZ. If both EMZs are occupied (one by each player), neither player can Summon from the Extra Deck unless they have a Link Monster pointing to a Main Monster Zone.
- If the opponent has a monster in the EMZ, you cannot attack directly — you must destroy all their monsters first including the one in the EMZ.
- Link Monsters cannot be in Defense Position and cannot be flipped face-down.
- If you take control of an opponent's monster in the EMZ, it moves to your Main Monster Zone.

## Board position

- The 2 EMZs appear at the top-center of the combined field (above both players' Main Monster Zones).
